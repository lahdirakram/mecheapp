 LOU — RUNWAY PACK (Gen-4)  ·  Mèche
 Prompts prêts à coller. L'ordre compte : (A) génère l'image de Lou,
 (B) charge-la comme Reference, (C) colle les prompts vidéo plan par plan.
 Les prompts visuels sont en ANGLAIS (Runway marche mieux ainsi).
 Le texte/voix-off FR est listé à part, à ajouter au montage.
=====================================================================


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 A. IMAGE DE RÉFÉRENCE — génère Lou d'abord (Runway Image / Gen-4 Image)
    Objectif : 1 portrait net, neutre, qui servira de Reference à tous
    les plans vidéo. Format 9:16 pour l'app, 16:9 pour le web.
---------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT (image):
Portrait of Lou, a warm and approachable hairstylist in her late
twenties, friendly confident smile, natural relaxed expression,
shoulder-length wavy brown hair, minimal makeup, wearing a simple
modern stylist apron over a plain top. Soft natural window light,
clean light studio background, shallow depth of field, photorealistic,
editorial beauty photography, eye-level, looking at camera.

NEGATIVE / À ÉVITER:
cartoon, plastic skin, heavy makeup, exaggerated, distorted hands,
text, logos, watermark.

ASTUCE: garde la MÊME seed et la MÊME image de référence pour tous les
plans, sinon le visage change d'un plan à l'autre.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 B. RÉGLAGES RUNWAY (à faire une fois)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Charge l'image A dans "References" (tag: @lou).
- Aspect ratio : 9:16 (app / stories) ou 16:9 (site / YouTube).
- Durée : 5s ou 10s par plan.
- Réutilise @lou dans CHAQUE prompt vidéo pour garder le même visage.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 C. PLANS VIDÉO — copie/colle un bloc par génération
---------------------------------------------------------------------

PLAN 1 — Hook / accroche (5s)
@lou looks directly into the camera with a friendly knowing smile,
soft natural light, clean bright studio, subtle slow push-in on her
face, she tilts her head slightly as if about to share a secret,
photorealistic, shallow depth of field, warm inviting mood.

PLAN 2 — Le selfie (5s)
@lou holds a smartphone at arm's length toward a young woman off
camera, gesturing warmly to take a selfie, natural window light, bright
modern space, gentle handheld camera, she smiles encouragingly,
photorealistic, candid lifestyle feel.

PLAN 3 — L'aperçu magique (5s)
Close-up of a smartphone screen showing a woman's selfie transforming
into a new hairstyle, smooth morph from before to after, @lou's hand
holding the phone, soft reflections, clean UI glow, shallow depth of
field, satisfying reveal, photorealistic.

PLAN 4 — La réaction (5s)
A young woman looks at her phone and breaks into a delighted surprised
smile, touching her hair, @lou beside her sharing the excitement, warm
natural light, bright salon-like space, gentle handheld camera,
authentic joyful moment, photorealistic.

PLAN 5 — Diversité des looks (5s)
Quick series of confident portraits of different people of varied skin
tones, hair textures and ages, each with a fresh stylish haircut,
turning toward camera and smiling, clean bright background, soft light,
upbeat rhythm, photorealistic editorial beauty.

PLAN 6 — Signature / fin (5s)
@lou gives a warm confident nod to the camera and a small inviting
gesture, soft natural light, clean minimal background with space for a
logo, slow gentle push-in, photorealistic, calm friendly close.
