FAQ

The questions we get.

Is the render actually realistic?+

Yes. Mèche uses a model trained on real cuts, not avatars. It keeps your skin, your light, your features, and only changes the hair. You can compare before and after in the app. If a render looks off, report it and we fix it.

The business model, plainly?+

Your first try is free. After that, you buy AI credits: 1 credit = 1 photo generated in HD, exported without watermark. Three packs: 5 credits at €0.99, 20 credits at €2.99 (most popular), 50 credits at €5.99 (best value). No subscription, no monthly charge, no card required for the free try. Purchased credits never expire.

Does it work on curly / coily / textured hair?+

Yes. We trained the model on every hair type (1A to 4C), every skin tone, every gender. If a texture or color doesn't render well, write to us, we iterate.

What about the "book a stylist" part?+

It's coming soon. For now, once you've picked your cut, you download the image and show it to your stylist. In-app booking is in the works: finding a stylist near you who can do your look, with their book and their slots.

Which countries?+

The try-on works everywhere in the world, right now. In-app booking will land first in France (Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, Lille, Nantes), then Italy and Spain.
